
# Retail Sales & Customer Analytics

**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, SQL (SQLite), Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to understand business performance, customer purchasing behaviour, and product trends. It uses Python for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis, SQL for extracting meaningful business insights, and Power BI for creating interactive dashboards.

The main objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support better business decision-making.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Track key business metrics and trends.
- Present insights through interactive dashboards.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data processing and analysis.
- **Pandas:** Data cleaning and manipulation.
- **SQLite:** Database management and SQL-based analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Load the retail sales dataset.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Handle missing values, duplicates and inconsistent records using Python.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Explore sales, customer and product trends.
4. **SQL Analysis:** Execute queries to calculate business metrics and identify important patterns.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Create interactive Power BI visualizations to monitor key performance indicators.
6. **Business Insights:** Interpret the results and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Key Analysis Areas

- Sales and revenue trends
- Product and category performance
- Regional sales comparison
- Customer purchasing patterns
- High-performing products and customers
- Business KPI monitoring

## Expected Outcomes

- Better understanding of retail sales performance.
- Identification of profitable products and important customer segments.
- Insights into regional and product-level trends.
- Interactive reporting to support data-driven decisions.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python, SQL and Power BI can be combined to analyze retail data, identify business trends and convert raw information into actionable business insights.
